Play Free Online Tic-Tac-Toe Game

Enjoy the classic game of Tic-Tac-Toe directly in your browser. Practice your skills against our advanced Minimax Artificial Intelligence algorithm, play pass-and-play on a single device, or challenge friends live online with real-time peer-to-peer rooms.

How to Play Online Multiplayer

  • Select vs Player (Online) in the mode menu.
  • Click Host Room to generate a unique 6-character room code.
  • Share your code with a friend, or have them enter it into the Room Code box and click Join.
  • Moves automatically sync in real-time between devices!

Tic-Tac-Toe Winning Strategies

  • Claim the Center: Securing the center cell gives you control over the highest number of winning lines.
  • Occupy Corners: Claiming corners creates double-threat setups, forcing your opponent into defensive blocks.
