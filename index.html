How to Play Tic-Tac-Toe

  • The game is played on a 3x3 grid.
  • Players take turns placing their chosen symbols in any empty space.
  • The first player to align three of their symbols horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins the match.
  • If all nine grid spaces are filled without three symbols aligned, the game ends in a tie.

Tic-Tac-Toe Winning Strategies

To maximize your chances of winning or securing a draw against any player:

  • Claim the Center: Securing the center cell gives you control over the highest number of winning lines.
  • Occupy Corners: Claiming corners creates double-threat setups, forcing your opponent into defensive blocks.
  • Block Threats: Pay close attention to your opponent's moves to block them whenever they get two symbols in a row.
